The website rental trap

A lot of small businesses in Omaha choose lower monthly website plans because they seem affordable upfront. The website looks fine at first, but later many business owners realize they cannot fully edit the site, do not have admin access, or are tied into ongoing fees they did not expect.

For some businesses, 12 months of payments could have already covered the cost of owning a website outright. Your website is a business asset — understanding ownership, access, and long-term control matters more than most people realize.

The offer usually sounds simple: a low upfront cost, a monthly payment, and someone else handles everything. After a year or two of monthly payments, questions start showing up: Can I move my site? Do I own the domain? Why can’t I log in? What happens if I cancel?

Before moving forward, ask:

  • Do I own the domain?
  • Will I have full admin access?
  • Can I move the site later?
  • What happens if I cancel?
  • Do I get backups and ownership of the files?
