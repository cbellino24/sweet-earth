Starting out

From idea to a real brand

If you are still building the business in your head, this is usually where it starts to feel real. You need a name that holds up, a logo you are proud of, and clean files you can hand to a printer, a sign shop, or a website without starting over every time.

We help Omaha, Papillion, and Lincoln founders shape those ideas into a brand identity you can actually use, from first sketches through the final logo package.

How we work

From first conversation to finished brand files

Four clear stages for Omaha-area businesses — you know what is due, when you review concepts, and what lands in your inbox.

  1. 01

    Learn

    We review what you sell, who you serve, and where the brand must work: storefront, vehicles, website, and social.

  2. 02

    Design

    Logo rounds, supporting graphics, and practical checks — small sizes, one-color versions, truck doors, and phones.

  3. 03

    Hand off

    Final exports, labeled folders, and short usage notes so your staff and vendors can implement without calling again.

    Already in business

    Need a higher resolution logo, a refresh, or sharper branding?

    Maybe the print shop asked for a vector file you do not have. Maybe your logo looks soft on a truck wrap, a banner, or a storefront sign. Or the brand just feels dated and you want a cleaner refresh without starting from zero.

    We rebuild and upgrade logos into high resolution files built for print and web, tighten colors and type, and raise the overall quality so your branding looks intentional everywhere a customer sees it.
