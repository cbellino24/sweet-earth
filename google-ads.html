FAQ

Google Ads management questions

How much should a small business spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your industry, service area, and how competitive the keywords are. A practical way to start is a budget you can run consistently for a few weeks, then scale based on tracked calls and leads.

Do Google Ads work for local businesses?

Yes — especially for services people search when they need help fast. Campaigns need intent targeting, local settings, and tracking. Without those, accounts often spend without learning anything useful.

Should I send ads to my homepage or a landing page?

For many local lead campaigns, a landing page works better because it matches the search and makes calls or quote requests simple. We recommend the right destination based on your offer.

Can you manage ads and build the landing page?

Yes. We are a web studio that also manages PPC, so we can handle the ads, the landing page, and conversion tracking together.

How long does it take to see results?

You can often see traffic quickly, but meaningful optimization takes time. The first weeks are about finding what you are paying for, tightening negatives, and improving the page or message.

Do you guarantee leads?

No. Anyone promising guaranteed leads is not being honest about auctions, competition, and conversion. What we guarantee is clean tracking, clear communication, and consistent optimization based on data.
