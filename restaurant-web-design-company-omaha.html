FAQ

Restaurant web design questions

Do I have to pay monthly for a restaurant website?

No. We can build a restaurant website as a standalone project with no forced monthly website payment. Monthly support is optional for updates, promos, or marketing.

Do you only do restaurant websites?

Restaurant web design is the focus here, but we can also help with branding and collateral like menus, flyers, and promo graphics as add-ons when it makes sense.

Can you redesign our current restaurant website?

Yes. We often rebuild outdated sites, simplify the menu experience, and make it easier for guests to call, reserve, or order on mobile.

Can you help us rank for happy hour and lunch searches?

Yes. We build dedicated pages for happy hour, lunch menus, and seasonal promos with headings and copy that match what people type into Google.

Do you build pages for holidays and big event weekends?

Yes — Valentine’s dinner, Mother’s Day brunch, game day, and other busy weekends deserve their own clear page with hours, menus, and a reservation or call button.

Can you align our website with our Google Business Profile?

Yes. We keep hours, location, and menu messaging consistent between your site and Google so guests see the same story on the homepage or map listing.
